Fields to know:

- `rank`: The product's rank among the best sellers in that category.
- `rating`: Star rating, out of 5.
- `reviews`: How many reviews the rating is based on.

## Options

| Option | What it does | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `country` | Two-letter country code, like `us`. | One of 21 values, like `us`, `gb` and `de`. |

## Cost and limits

- **Price:** 2 credits per call. Failed calls and empty results cost nothing.
- **Paging:** None. One call returns everything for the input.
