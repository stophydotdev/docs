## Response

`data.results` lists the images, each with its full-size link and the page it comes from. Here is a sample response, shortened: lists show one row and long text is cut.

Fields to know:

- `position`: Where the result sits in the list, starting at 1.

## Options

| Option | What it does | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `country` | Two-letter country code, like `us`. | Two letters. |
| `language` | Two-letter language code, like `en` or `pt`. | Two letters. |
| `size` | Only images of this size. | `large`, `medium` or `icon`. |
| `color` | Only images in this color. | One of 14 values, like `blackAndWhite`, `transparent` and `red`. |
| `type` | The kind of image. | `clipArt`, `lineDrawing` or `gif`. |
| `time` | Only images from this period. | `past24Hours`, `pastWeek`, `pastMonth` or `pastYear`. |
| `usageRights` | Only images with this license. | `creativeCommons` or `commercial`. |
| `limit` | Return at most this many images. You pay 1 credit per 10 returned. | Whole number, 1 to 100. |

## Cost and limits

- **Price:** 1 credit per 10 images. A call costs 10 credits at most. Failed calls and empty results cost nothing. Send `limit` to get fewer results and pay for fewer.
- **Paging:** None. One call returns everything for the input.
