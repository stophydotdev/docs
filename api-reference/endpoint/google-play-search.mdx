## Options

| Option | What it does | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `country` | Two-letter country code, like `us`. | Two letters. Default `us`. |
| `language` | Language code, like `en` or `pt-BR`. | Text. Default `en`. |
| `limit` | Return at most this many apps. You pay 1 credit per 10 returned. | Whole number, 1 to 50. |

## Cost and limits

- **Price:** 1 credit per 10 apps. A call costs 5 credits at most. Failed calls and empty results cost nothing. Send `limit` to get fewer results and pay for fewer.
- **Paging:** None. One call returns everything for the input.
