- **Endpoint:** `google.search`
- **Returns:** `data` has `aiOverview`, `knowledgeGraph`, `results`, `peopleAlsoAsk`, `relatedSearches`, `isPartial`.
- **Cost:** 1 credit per 10 results returned, never more than you asked for (10 credits at most). Failed calls cost nothing.
- **Paging:** No.

Filter with `within`, `country`, `language`, and domains to include or exclude. Keys a result does not have are left out. Without a key you get up to 10 results.
