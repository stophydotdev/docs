Fields to know:

- `rating`: Star rating, out of 5.
- `reviews`: How many reviews the rating is based on.
- `sold`: How many units were sold.

## Options

| Option | What it does | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cursor` | The `cursor` from the previous response. Send it back unchanged. | Text from the previous response. |

## Cost and limits

- **Price:** 1 credit per call. Failed calls and empty results cost nothing.
- **Paging:** Send the `cursor` from the response to get the next page, until a response has none.
