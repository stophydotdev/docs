- **Endpoint:** `transcript`
- **Returns:** `data` has `videoId`, `videoUrl`, `language`, `isAutoGenerated`, `durationSeconds`, `transcribedSeconds`, `text`, `segments`.
- **Cost:** 2 credits when the video has captions; otherwise 2 credits plus 1 credit for every 10 seconds of audio, up to 30 minutes (182 credits at most). Failed calls cost nothing.
- **Paging:** No.

Pass a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram link in `video`. Set `includeTimestamps` to get each line with its time. When there are no captions, the audio is transcribed and `transcribedSeconds` says how much. TikTok videos over 3 minutes are refused, and refused calls cost nothing.
