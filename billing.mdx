Stophy uses credits. You buy credits ahead of time and each successful call uses some.

## Price

- 1,000 credits cost $2.
- New accounts start with 500 free credits.
- Top up at [stophy.dev](https://stophy.dev). The smallest top-up is $5.

## What a call costs

Each endpoint has a cost in credits. Find it in the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction), on each source page, or in `GET https://api.stophy.dev/v1/endpoints` (`credits` and `perItems`).

- Most endpoints cost a fixed number of credits per call.
- Some charge per group of results. When `perItems` is set, a call costs `credits` for every `perItems` items it returns, and at least `credits`. For example, web search costs 1 credit per 10 results, so 25 results cost 3 credits.
- Failed calls cost nothing.

## See what a call cost

Every successful response tells you:

- `creditsUsed` in the JSON body
- the `x-credits-used` header, also on markdown responses

For your balance and history, use [`GET /v1/usage`](/api-reference/usage) and [`GET /v1/logs`](/api-reference/logs).

## Running out

When your balance is too low for a call, it answers `402` with code `insufficientCredits` and nothing is charged. Top up and try again.
