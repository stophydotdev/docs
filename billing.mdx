Stophy uses credits. You buy credits ahead of time and each successful call uses some.

## Price

| You add | Price per 1,000 credits |
| --- | --- |
| Less than $100 | $1.50 |
| $100 or more | $1.20 |
| $500 or more | $0.80 |

- New accounts start with 1,000 free credits.
- Top up at [stophy.dev](https://stophy.dev). The smallest top-up is $10.
- Credits never expire.

## What a call costs

Every endpoint has a fixed price per call, from 1 to 4 credits, except `transcript`, which depends on the video (see below). The price does not change with how many results come back. Errors and empty results cost nothing.

| Cost | Endpoints |
| --- | --- |
| 5 credits | `googletravel.flights`, `ads.search`, `ads.ad`, `meta.ads.page`, `walmart.search` |
| 3 credits | `maps.search`, `airbnb.search`, `zillow.search`, `rightmove.search`, `immoscout.search`, `upwork.search`, `linkedin.jobs.search`, `walmart.product` |
| 2 credits | `instagram.profile`, `zillow.property` |
| 1 credit | Everything else, including every Reddit, Indeed, Tripadvisor, App Store and Google Play endpoint, and `ads.advertisers` |
| From 2 credits | `transcript`, see below |

One call returns one page. To get the next page, send the `cursor` from the response in a new call, which costs the same again and continues with no gaps. Find an endpoint's cost in the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction), on each source page, or in `credits` in `GET https://api.stophy.dev/v1/endpoints`.

Failed calls cost nothing. A call that Stophy refuses, for example a TikTok video over 3 minutes sent to `transcript`, costs nothing either.

### Transcript

`transcript` costs 2 credits when the video has captions. When it has none, the audio is transcribed instead and the call costs 2 credits plus 1 credit for every 10 seconds of audio, up to 30 minutes, so 182 credits at most. Audio is rounded up to the next 10 seconds. A video without captions that is longer than 30 minutes is refused and costs nothing.

The response has `transcribedSeconds` when the audio was transcribed, and `creditsUsed` has what the call cost.

| Video | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Has captions | 2 |
| No captions, 60 seconds | 8 |
| No captions, 5 minutes | 32 |
| No captions, 30 minutes | 182 |

The catalog at `GET https://api.stophy.dev/v1/endpoints` has `credits` for the lowest price and `pricing` for the terms when the price varies.

## See what a call cost

Every successful response tells you:

- `creditsUsed` in the JSON body
- the `x-credits-used` header

For your balance and history, use [`GET /v1/usage`](/api-reference/usage) and [`GET /v1/logs`](/api-reference/logs).

## Running out

When your balance is too low for a call, it answers `402` with code `insufficientCredits` and nothing is charged. The error says how many credits the call needs and how many you have. Top up and try again.
