Run one command, then sign in with your browser. No key to copy.

<McpClientSelector variant="signin" showSeeAll={false} />

`https://mcp.stophy.dev/mcp` is a server URL for your client, not a page to open. Your client starts the sign-in.

## What happens

1. Your client opens stophy.dev in your browser.
2. You sign in or sign up. New accounts get 1,000 free credits.
3. Stophy shows which app is asking. Approve it only if you started the connection.
4. Your client connects. Calls use your credits.

Signing out of stophy.dev disconnects the app. Your client asks you to sign in again next time.

## Check it

Ask your agent this.

```text
Find coffee shops in Austin with a rating above 4.5 and list their websites.
```
