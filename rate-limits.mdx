---
title: "Rate limits"
description: "How fast you can call Stophy."
---

| | With a key | Without a key |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requests | 600 a minute per account | 10 a minute and 50 a day, per IP |
| Running at once | 10 per account | |
| `limit` input | Up to the endpoint's maximum | At most 10 |
| Paging | Yes | First page only |

Every call answers or fails within 25 seconds.

## Headers

Responses include:

- `X-RateLimit-Limit`: your limit
- `X-RateLimit-Remaining`: what is left

## When you go over

| Code | Status | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rateLimited` | `429` | Wait for the `Retry-After` header (also `retryAfterSeconds` in the body), then retry. |
| `tooManyInFlight` | `429` | Too many of your calls are running at once. Wait for one to finish, then retry. |

Both are marked `retryable: true`. See [Errors](/api-reference/errors) for a retry loop.
