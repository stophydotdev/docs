<Tip>YouTube search, video details and transcripts work without a key. A YouTube transcript costs 1 credit and comes from the captions. TikTok and Instagram transcripts cost 1 credit when the video has captions and 2 credits plus 1 credit per 10 seconds of audio when it does not: see [Credits and billing](/billing). TikTok videos over 3 minutes can't be transcribed. YouTube charts cost 1 credit per 10 results.</Tip>
