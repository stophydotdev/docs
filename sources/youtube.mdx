## Endpoints

| Endpoint | Cost | Paging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [`youtube.search`](/api-reference/endpoint/youtube-search) | 1 credit per 20 items, at least 1 credit | Yes |
| [`youtube.video`](/api-reference/endpoint/youtube-video) | 1 credit | No |
| [`youtube.transcript`](/api-reference/endpoint/youtube-transcript) | 3 credits | No |
| [`youtube.comments`](/api-reference/endpoint/youtube-comments) | 1 credit per 20 items, at least 1 credit | Yes |
| [`youtube.comments.replies`](/api-reference/endpoint/youtube-comments-replies) | 1 credit per 10 items, at least 1 credit | Yes |
| [`youtube.channel`](/api-reference/endpoint/youtube-channel) | 1 credit per 30 items, at least 1 credit | Yes |
| [`youtube.playlist`](/api-reference/endpoint/youtube-playlist) | 1 credit per 100 items, at least 1 credit | Yes |

## Example: youtube.search

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.stophy.dev/v1/youtube/search \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $STOPHY_API_KEY" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"query":"rust tutorial","limit":2}'
```
